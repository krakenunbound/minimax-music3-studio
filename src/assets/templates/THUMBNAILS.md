# Music 3 template thumbnails

These twelve square illustrations were generated specifically for MiniMax Music 3 Studio. The optimized 320×320 WebP files beside this document are used by the template browser. Full-resolution PNG masters are kept in `full/` for future larger template views.

Every image used the same visual constraints: square editorial album-art thumbnail, one strong focal subject, rich cinematic lighting, readable at small size, no words, no lettering, no logo, and no watermark.

## Final prompt set

- **Cinematic alt rock** — Empty warehouse rehearsal space with a lone microphone stand and worn electric guitar, blue-hour window light cut by a restrained red stage glow, intimate tension and cathartic potential.
- **Dark synth-pop** — Silhouetted female singer behind rain-streaked glass, magenta waveform reflections against deep indigo shadows, cool nocturnal intimacy and restrained industrial geometry.
- **Nordic ritual folk** — Weathered hide drum, carved wooden ritual staff and antler on frost-dark earth between misty standing stones, cold dawn, austere firelight, raw handmade materials.
- **Ambient trance** — Concentric violet and cyan light rings floating above a calm midnight ocean, distant horizon and soft stars, weightless meditative depth with no figures.
- **Modern metal** — Black seven-string guitar embedded in fractured volcanic rock, subtle molten amber fissures, steel-blue smoke and hard directional light, physical rather than fantasy-heavy.
- **Synthwave** — Low retro sports car on a neon grid highway, huge pink sun sinking behind a dark city, electric blue and magenta reflections, cinematic night-drive momentum.
- **Acoustic bluegrass** — Old banjo and fiddle resting together on an Appalachian porch at golden hour, worn wood, distant blue mountains and honest natural warmth.
- **Deep house relax** — Turquoise vinyl turntable on a moonlit rooftop, soft city bokeh, dark teal atmosphere and one restrained violet accent, quiet late-night focus.
- **Brazilian phonk** — Extreme low-angle motorcycle rear wheel throwing orange sparks across a rain-wet São Paulo night street, acid-green city reflections and aggressive forward motion.
- **Lo-fi study beats** — Cozy worn study desk with headphones, open notebook, steaming mug and amber lamp beside a rainy night window, intimate hand-painted texture.
- **Heroic anime opening** — Original caped hero silhouette on a city rooftop at explosive sunrise, cobalt sky and coral clouds, determined upward composition with no franchise symbols.
- **Afrobeats sunset** — Two carved hand drums on coral fabric beside a turquoise ocean at golden sunset, palm shadows and rich handcrafted wood, warm communal energy.
